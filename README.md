## 🎯 What is Café Manager?

**Café Manager** is a business-management system designed around the real workflow of a café.

It separates responsibilities between:

- 👑 **Admin** — supervision, sales, staff, declarations and reporting
- 🍸 **Barman** — stock, merchandise reception and cash declarations
- 📱 **Server** — fast order entry and daily declarations

The system is designed to make café operations more transparent and easier to monitor.

---

## 🚀 Main Product Capabilities

### 👑 Admin

- Monitor sales and operational activity
- Review daily declarations
- Compare declared amounts
- Manage staff and roles
- Review merchandise reports
- Supervise inventory and business activity

### 🍸 Barman

- Record received merchandise
- Manage stock-related workflows
- Submit daily cash declarations
- Create merchandise reports
- Work with role-specific access

### 📱 Server

- Create customer orders
- Work from a mobile-friendly interface
- Submit daily declarations
- Select the active barman for declaration workflows
- Track operational activity during the day

---

## 🧠 Business Problem

Traditional café management often depends on paper notes, manual calculations and disconnected workflows.

Café Manager is designed to reduce that friction by bringing together:

- sales tracking
- staff accountability
- inventory control
- cash declarations
- daily closing
- operational reporting

into one centralized system.
